# Jane Farley / Remi Ganth

Jane Farley suffers from a split personality disorder. 
As far as she knows, she lives a perfectly normal, if slightly disappointing life, 
though can never seem to get enough sleep. 
Sometimes things also go missing or turn up in weird places she doesn’t remember 
putting them. One time she thinks she woke up somewhere she didn’t remember going, 
but that might just have been an oddly vivid dream.

She spends most of her time working at the local tavern, usually helping in the kitchens, 
but on special occasions she puts on a bit of a show as a barista.

In the evenings, she trains at the monastery on the nearby mountain. 
She was raised there after being abandoned by her parents as a baby. 
Sometimes, it feels like she’s nothing but a routine. 
Sometimes it feels like that’s the only thing keeping her together. 
They often let her stay the night, though she tries not to lean on their generosity too often.
